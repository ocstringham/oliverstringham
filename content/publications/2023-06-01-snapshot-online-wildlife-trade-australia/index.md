## Abstract

Online marketplaces have become a major venue for the pet trade, yet the scale and composition of this trade remains poorly documented in many countries, including Australia. We used automated web scrapers to collect pet trade advertisements across 12 Australian e-commerce websites over a 14-week period, identifying 1,192 species being traded, including 667 non-native species and 81 species of conservation concern. Our results provide a baseline snapshot of Australia's online pet trade and highlight the need for ongoing monitoring to detect biosecurity and conservation risks as they emerge.
