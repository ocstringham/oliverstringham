## Abstract

Online plant trade is a growing pathway for the introduction and spread of invasive species, but ongoing monitoring across the many platforms involved is limited. We used automated web scraping to capture snapshots of online plant trade across 11 Australian e-commerce websites between 2020 and 2023, identifying 3,822 species for sale. We categorized these species by their Australian regulatory, naturalisation, and native status to flag species posing emerging invasion risk, and identified the platforms carrying the highest concentration of high-risk listings — providing a prioritized target list for future monitoring and intervention.
